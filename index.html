Privacy Policy for Merge Horizons

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Information We Collect

Merge Horizons does not currently require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location data.

The current version of Merge Horizons does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal information on behalf of Yazgan Games.

Children's Privacy

Merge Horizons does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
