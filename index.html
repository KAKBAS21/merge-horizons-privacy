Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Merge Horizons: World Tour is developed and published by Yazgan Games. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

Information You Provide

Merge Horizons does not require users to create an account. Yazgan Games does not directly request personal information such as your name, phone number, precise location, or account credentials in order to play the game.

Game progress and game settings are primarily stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Merge Horizons includes optional rewarded advertisements. Players may choose to watch an advertisement in exchange for optional in-game rewards such as Energy or Gold.

The game uses Google Mobile Ads, including Google AdMob, to provide advertising services.

Third-Party Services and Data

Third-party advertising services used by Merge Horizons may automatically collect certain information when advertisements are requested or displayed.

Depending on the user's device, region, privacy settings, consent choices, and advertising configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising-related identifiers where permitted
  • Advertising and ad interaction information
  • App interaction information
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information
  • Performance information

This information may be used by advertising providers for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and improving their services.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Merge Horizons uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to request and manage privacy and advertising consent choices.

Depending on your region and applicable law, you may be shown privacy choices before advertising services are activated. Where required, you may also be able to review or change your privacy choices from within the game.

Personalized Advertising

The availability of personalized or non-personalized advertising depends on your region, consent choices, device settings, and the requirements of the advertising provider.

Children's Privacy

Merge Horizons does not knowingly request personal information directly from children.

Third-party services used by the game may process limited device or advertising-related information as described above and in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

Data Security

Yazgan Games takes reasonable steps to limit the information directly collected by the game. Third-party service providers are responsible for protecting data processed through their services according to their own security practices and policies.
